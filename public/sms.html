Personal SMS Reminders

This is a personal automation project run by one person, Brandon Strain. It is not a business and it does not send marketing or promotional messages.

What it does

I text a phone number to add a to-do item or set a reminder in my own Notion workspace. The number texts me back to confirm what was saved, and texts me again when a reminder is due.

Who receives messages

Only me, the owner of the number. The service only responds to my own phone number and ignores texts from anyone else. No other person is ever messaged, and no contact lists or purchased lists are used.

Opt-in

I opt in by texting the number first. Every message the service sends is a direct reply to my own text or a reminder I created myself.

Message types and frequency

Confirmations such as "Reminder set: call mom, Wed, Oct 14, 3:00 PM" and reminder alerts such as "Reminder: call mom". Roughly 5 to 20 messages per day at most. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help.

Privacy

The text of my messages is stored only in my own Notion workspace, and is processed by the services used to send and receive texts. No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Terms

This service is for personal use by its owner only. It is provided as-is with no guarantee of delivery.
